MacAngioView is now

CardiacStudy: DICOM Viewer

Play cath and echo cines at their acquired frame rate, step through CT and MR, and read the study's reports and ECG in the same window. Now for Mac and Windows.

The MacAngioView beta is over. Its successor is a new app, rebuilt from scratch for each platform, and available only from the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store.

Mac: macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon or Intel · Windows: Windows 10 (1809 or later) or 11, 64-bit

Everything MacAngioView did, and more

Open any study

Patient CDs, DICOMDIR folders or loose DICOM files. Every run, report and waveform in one strip, in one window.

Play it as acquired

Angiography, fluoroscopy and echo at their recorded frame rate, with DSA subtraction, window/level, zoom and pan.

CT and MR

Step through series slice by slice, with each slice's position on the study's scout image and landmark slices you can jump back to.

Reports and ECG

Structured and PDF reports, presentation states, and 12-lead ECG and hemodynamic waveforms as calibrated lead strips.

Annotate and measure

Stenosis and LV EF on angiograms; Doppler, 2D, Simpson's LV volumes and M-mode on echo; a measurement summary for the whole study.

Teach

Quiz Mode hides the report while you read the images cold and record your findings, then lets you compare. Export movies for lectures.

Private by design

Works offline with no account. The app makes no network connections, and nothing leaves your computer.

One purchase

Bought once from the store, with free updates. No subscription, no in-app purchases and no beta expiry.

Questions

What happened to MacAngioView?

The free MacAngioView beta has ended, on both Mac and Windows, and there will be no more betas. CardiacStudy: DICOM Viewer replaces it: a new app written for each platform, with everything MacAngioView did plus annotations and measurements. On the Microsoft Store it is called CardiacStudy Player, which is also the name the installed app uses on both.

Will my installed beta keep working?

Yes, until its expiry date. Beta builds are no longer available to download and will not be updated.

How do I get it?

Only from the Mac App Store or the Microsoft Store. It is a one-time purchase with free updates.

Can I use it for diagnosis?

No. It is for teaching and case review. It is not a certified medical device and must not be used for diagnosis or clinical decisions.

Support: questions and feedback to support@williamshillsoftware.com. Please never send patient images, discs or files that identify a patient.
